Privacy policy

Applies to Resonant Tuner for Windows.

Summary

Resonant Tuner works entirely on your device. It does not record audio, upload anything, create accounts or collect analytics.

Microphone

The app listens to the selected microphone or audio input only to detect the pitch of the sound you play. Audio is analysed in memory and discarded immediately. It is never saved to disk or sent anywhere. The input is released when you close the app.

Data we collect

None. The app has no network features, no accounts, no advertising and no telemetry.

Data stored on your device

Settings (such as language, reference pitch and device choice) and custom tunings last for the current session only. Nothing is written to disk by the app.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any. It is open source and its components are listed in the third-party notices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised as an issue at github.com/tinodo/Resonant-Tuner/issues.
